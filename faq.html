FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Straight answers for confident next steps.

Open any question to understand the platform more clearly and keep your learning moving forward.

01What is ClickBaitPays?+

ClickBaitPays is a paid-to-click advertising platform that connects advertisers seeking traffic with participating members who view ads through active Ad Campaigns.

02Is ClickBaitPays a scam or Ponzi scheme?+

ClickBaitPays operates through identifiable advertising services, daily ad-viewing activity, campaign tools, and an optional one-level affiliate program. MTG uses the platform every day as a member and affiliate. It is not presented here as an investment or guaranteed-income product.

03What does Bhanoyi mean?+

Bhanoyi is a Zulu word used here to mean a scam or fraudulent scheme. It is included as a cultural definition, not a formal legal category.

04Is ClickBaitPays an MLM?+

ClickBaitPays uses a one-level direct affiliate program rather than a traditional structure that rewards multiple downline generations. This explains the design difference, not a regulatory classification.

05Are referrals required?+

No. Referrals are not required to participate through your own campaign activity. The affiliate program is an additional, optional path.

06What is web traffic?+

Web traffic means people visiting a website or online destination. Traffic can create awareness and possible action, but it does not guarantee a sale, registration, or other conversion.

07How can ClickBaitPays help an advertiser get traffic?+

An advertiser can create an approved Ad Campaign linking to something already being promoted. Participating viewers see the ad and may visit the destination. The campaign supplies exposure, while the offer, message, page quality, relevance, and follow-up influence what happens next.

08What is the difference between advertising and marketing?+

Advertising is paid exposure. Marketing is the wider process that includes understanding the audience, shaping the offer, communicating value, building trust, supporting conversion, and retaining customers. Advertising is one part of marketing.

09Is account registration free?+

The official registration is free. Paid campaign activity requires an Ad Campaign purchase and the one-time activation fee for the chosen level.

10How many campaigns can one account run?+

One account can run up to three active Ad Campaigns at one time, regardless of level combination.

11Should every learner aim for three Level 7 campaigns?+

No. Three campaigns and Level 7 are optional possibilities, not expected destinations. Any decision should follow learning, current-rule verification, affordability, risk tolerance, and experience with the process.

12How long before campaign funds become available?+

A campaign includes 12 click days followed by a fixed seven-day hold before release to Available Balance.

13What withdrawal fee and timing apply?+

The current process includes a 10% withdrawal fee, one request per week, and manual fulfillment stated as within 48 hours. Check the platform before requesting in case the rules have changed.

14How many accounts may a household have?+

Up to three accounts for different adult household members. They must use the same original sponsor, and household members may not sponsor one another.

15Is this the official ClickBaitPays website?+

No. This is an independent affiliate education website created by MTG. Official platform pages are clearly marked and open on the clickbaitpays.me domain.

16Does this website save my course progress in an account?+

No account is created. Quiz progress and theme preference are stored only in the current browser and can be reset or cleared at any time.
