What Happens During and After the 12 Click Days?

Understand daily ad activity, campaign completion, the fixed hold, and Available Balance as separate stages.

611

Campaign activity and fund availability do not happen at the same time. Understanding the sequence helps prevent unrealistic expectations.

01

TIMELINE INFOGRAPHIC

The campaign lifecycle

Day 1Begin daily adsView each required ad for 15 seconds.
Days 2 to 12Continue activityAds reset at midnight Panama time, UTC-5.
After day 12Fixed holdThe hold lasts seven days.
After releaseAvailable BalanceUse the options permitted by current platform rules.

Daily ad counts vary

Levels 1 to 3 require three ads per day. Level 4 requires five, Level 5 requires six, Level 6 requires ten, and Level 7 requires twenty. Each ad runs for 15 seconds.

Reset time matters

The daily cycle resets at midnight in Panama, UTC-5. Convert that time carefully for your location and check the dashboard for any current changes.

See the daily workflow in learning order: open Tutorial 4: How to View Ads and Earn Daily.

No guaranteed timing beyond the stated rules. Platform processing, maintenance, compliance review, and rule changes can affect real-world experiences.

Key takeaways

  • ✓Complete the required ads on each of 12 click days.
  • ✓Campaign completion is followed by a fixed seven-day hold.
  • ✓Available Balance is a later stage.
  • ✓Check your dashboard for current timing and account status.
