Understanding the Staggered Campaign Concept

Understand the three-active-campaign limit and how different start dates can create an organized, overlapping schedule.

811

One account may have up to three active Ad Campaigns under the current guide. Some members consider starting them at different times. This is a scheduling concept, not a guarantee of weekly payouts or consistent income.

The responsible way to think about it

  • ✓Understand one complete cycle first.
  • ✓Confirm you can manage the daily activity.
  • ✓Review actual experience before adding another campaign.
  • ✓Keep total spending within what you can afford to lose.

What to avoid

  • ×Borrowing to reach a higher level.
  • ×Treating illustrations as guaranteed cash flow.
  • ×Starting more campaigns than you can manage.
  • ×Ignoring rule changes or crypto risk.

Key takeaways

  • ✓The current guide allows up to three active campaigns per account.
  • ✓Campaigns may use different levels and start dates under current rules.
  • ✓Staggering describes timing, not guaranteed income.
  • ✓Review one cycle before considering a second or third campaign.
