Understand the account rules, registration steps, and practical habits that support a smooth start.

SAFETY INFOGRAPHIC

Four protection zones

PERSON:One account per individual

Each account holder must be an adult using accurate information.

HOUSEHOLD:Up to three adult accounts

Use the same original sponsor. Household members may not sponsor each other.

CONNECTION:Use the home IP to register

Official materials say not to use a VPN or mobile data during registration.

ACTIVITY:Participate manually

No proxies, bots, auto-clickers, scripts, emulators, masking, or automation.

Before taking action

  • ✓Check the current account rules.
  • ✓Confirm the sponsor and registration details.
  • ✓Choose only what fits your affordability and risk tolerance.
  • ✓Use money you can afford to lose.

Keep perspective

  • ✓Results are not guaranteed.
  • ✓Cryptocurrency carries transfer and value risk.
  • ✓Platform rules and figures can change.
  • ✓Past platform activity cannot guarantee future continuity.

Key takeaways

  • ✓Follow individual and household account limits.
  • ✓Register from the permitted connection without masking.
  • ✓Avoid prohibited automation and account tools.
  • ✓Make your own informed, affordable, and risk-aware decision.
