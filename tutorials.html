VIDEO COMING SOON03CAMPAIGNS

VIDEO 3

How to Buy an Ad Campaign and Activate a Level

Learn how to choose a Campaign Level, enter the permitted advertisement details, confirm the purchase, and pay the one-time activation fee when using that level for the first time.

  • Activation applies the first time you activate a particular level
  • Later campaigns at that already activated level do not require the activation fee again
  • Your advertisement is shown to members participating at the same Campaign Level

If you have active campaigns at multiple levels, each campaign has its own ad set. The Click Ads area uses separate campaign tabs so you can complete the required ads for each active campaign.

Compare first: understand Campaign Levels and ad visibility.

VIDEO 4

How to View Ads and Earn Daily

See how to open the Click Ads area, select an active campaign, view each available ad for the required time, return to the dashboard, and proceed through the full daily list.

  • The number of ads depends on the Campaign Level
  • Each active Ad Campaign has its own set of ads
  • Complete every active campaign's required ads for the day

Understand the lifecycle: read the daily clicks and earnings lesson.
