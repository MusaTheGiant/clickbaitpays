ClickBaitPays Campaign Levels 1 to 7 Explained

Read the campaign table correctly and distinguish campaign cost, activation, activity, completion value, and member amount.

511

Each level has a campaign cost, a one-time activation fee, required daily ads, a completion value, and a listed member amount. Higher levels involve larger purchases and more daily ads.

Review before choosing. Use these figures to understand how the campaign levels are structured. They are not guaranteed profits, typical results, or financial advice, and platform figures can change.

Read the first purchase correctly

The first total includes the campaign cost plus that level's activation fee. The activation fee is charged once for that level, not on every later campaign purchase at the same level.

Rounded per-click values

Some displayed per-click figures are rounded. Multiplying a rounded number may differ slightly from the official final member amount. Use the official completion figures for comparison.

Key takeaways

  • ✓First total and later campaign cost are different columns.
  • ✓Activation is once on the first purchase at that level.
  • ✓Higher levels require more money and may require more daily ads.
  • ✓Only choose a level after checking affordability, effort, risk, and current rules.
