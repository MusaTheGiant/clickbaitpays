YOUR 7-STEP ACTION PATH

Turn what you have learned into confident action.

Start where you are, use what you have, and build practical experience one manageable step at a time.

  1. 01

    Understand the model

    Complete the lessons, use the glossary, and strengthen any answer you missed.

  2. 02

    Choose what you want to promote

    Identify an existing website, offer, service, product, referral link, content item, or cause.

  3. 03

    Create your free account

    Register accurately through the official platform and confirm the correct sponsor before submitting.

  4. 04

    Start small and learn the workflow

    If you choose paid participation, select a manageable starting level and follow each step carefully.

  5. 05

    Complete one full campaign cycle

    Build real experience with campaign setup, daily activity, completion, the hold period, and Available Balance.

  6. 06

    Review, improve, and grow

    Use what you learned before deciding whether a second or third active campaign fits your plan.

  7. 07

    Share what you learned

    Help others understand traffic, campaigns, and responsible participation. Strong communities grow through useful knowledge.

THE HIGHEST CAMPAIGN LEVEL

Let experience lead the way to Level 7

Level 7 is the highest campaign level. Treat it as an advanced option, not a race. Build experience first and move only at a pace that fits your plan.

  • ✓Use your own available funds rather than borrowed money.
  • ✓Complete and understand one campaign before expanding.
  • ✓Match every level to your budget and daily workload.
  • ✓Use the three-active-campaign limit strategically.
