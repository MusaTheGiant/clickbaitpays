Login detail

The official member login asks for the username, not the email address. Use the official login URL and avoid look-alike pages.

Protect the security PIN

Treat the PIN like a password. Do not share it in groups, screenshots, forms, or messages. MTG will never need your private PIN to explain the platform.

Key takeaways

  • ✓Review payment and campaign details before confirming actions.
  • ✓Keep passwords, PINs, wallet keys, and recovery phrases private.
