PROJECTED TIMELINE

Start, completion and availability

The estimated Available Balance date follows 12 click days and a fixed seven-day hold. It is not a guaranteed withdrawal date. The website states that one withdrawal request may be submitted per week and manual fulfilment may take up to 48 hours after a permitted request.

How This Calculation Works +

The calculator uses the exact fixed package figures shown on the Home page and in Lesson 5. Daily, seven-day and 30-day member pace figures are prorated from the published member amount across 12 click days. They are comparison rates and are never added to the campaign's completion value.

  • Total capital: first total for the first purchase at a level, plus the later campaign cost for any additional campaign at that same level.
  • Gross completion value: the published completion value for each campaign.
  • Listed member amount: the published 90% member figure for each campaign.
  • Estimated net profit: listed member amount minus total capital committed.
  • Estimated ROI: estimated net profit divided by total capital, multiplied by 100.

Displayed per-click values are rounded on the website, so this calculator uses the published final member amounts rather than multiplying the rounded per-click figures.

How Staggered Campaigns Work +

Campaign 1 starts on Day 0, Campaign 2 on Day 7 and Campaign 3 on Day 14. Each campaign keeps the same individual return figures. Staggering changes timing and workload distribution only. It does not increase the earning rate or guarantee weekly withdrawals.
